Rooted in expertise.
Driven by connection.

SLQS Saudi Arabia is a professional community connecting Sri Lankan quantity surveying professionals working throughout the Kingdom.

Our platform is designed to encourage professional relationships, continuing learning and the exchange of industry experience. From emerging professionals to experienced practitioners, the focus is on participation, collaboration and shared growth.

Official association history, leadership information and milestones will be published here as they are confirmed by the SLQS committee.

OUR DIRECTION

What brings us together

01

Our vision

To be a connected and engaged community that supports Sri Lankan quantity surveying professionals across Saudi Arabia.

Our mission

To create opportunities for professional exchange, meaningful networking, knowledge sharing and community engagement.

Professional integrity, mutual respect, collaboration, continuous learning and a commitment to shared progress.
